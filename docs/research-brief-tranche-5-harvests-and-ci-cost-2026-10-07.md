## Executive Summary

The harvest closeout records 23 mechanism rows as `absorbed`, `intentionally_not_adopted`, `specialized_source_only`, `optional_interoperability` or `obsolete_or_superseded` with `unresolved_known_harvest_items: []` (fixture :376) and `runtime_dependency_required: false` on every row. Measured against what `AgentMemory.open()`/`recall()` can reach today, of the 21 rows that name a retrieval, ranking, currentness, consolidation, failure-memory or relation-propagation mechanism, **5 are shipped, 3 are declined with a technical reason, and 13 are tranches**: the code either lives in a library or harness the facade never calls (vector route, typed-graph route, controlled recall, metabolism, failure memory) or does not exist (JH-06 call/deadline budget, JH-12 typed probabilities, JH-13 decision cache, JH-16 consumer package, the entity/causal traversal the fixture claims). Two further drifts matter for planning: the published baseline records (v1, inherited by v2) claim `semantic_vector_retrieval`, `typed_relations_graph_traversal` and `memory_metabolism` as `native_qualified` although none of the three is reachable from the public facade; and the RC1 evidence closeout still asserts the harvest is `active_open` while the final fixture says `complete_exhaustive_closeout`. On CI: none of #662 Phases 2–4 has landed. Every PR head triggers about 86 workflow runs of which 41 are exact `push` duplicates of `pull_request` runs (about half of roughly 86 wall-minutes per head), every merge to `main` triggers 42 more, 73 of 85 workflow files have no `timeout-minutes`, no push or pull_request workflow has a `concurrency` block, and 20 jobs per head run the full `unittest discover` (22 passes). ## Recommendations

1. **(P1, #672 item 1) Reclassify the closeout as a versioned fixture, not an edit in place**: a new `reference/fixtures/harvest-closeout-final-v2.json` with a closed `disposition` enum `{shipped, tranche, declined}` plus `runtime_reachability` (`facade`, `library_only`, `harness_only`, `absent`), one row per mechanism in finding A (23 rows + the missing UOR saturation-decay row), each `shipped` row citing the module and the lane evidence id, each `tranche` row citing an issue and its lane gate, each `declined` row a technical reason; a test that reads the v2 fixture and, for every `shipped` row, asserts the cited module is importable and the cited evidence id exists in an integration's `evidence_history`; retire the `active_open` assertion in `test_rc1_evidence_closeout.py:40` to the v2 status.
